The first release of RipperPS. Everything I've been able to test on my own console works, but the drive you plug in decides what can be ripped, and I don't own every drive out there. So this one is as much about gathering data as it is about ripping. If you have a drive with Kreon, OmniDrive or LibreDrive firmware, I'd love to hear from you.

### New

- Rip from the PS5's own Blu-ray drive and USB CD, DVD and Blu-ray drives, more than one at a time.
- Games: PS1 and PS2 to BIN/CUE, CHD or ISO, PS3 discs to ISO (whole or split for FAT32) or a game folder, decrypted with a key file.
- PS4 and PS5 game dumps from installed games, with their update and DLC, as a folder, `.ffpkg` or `.exfat`. PS4 games can also come out as a fake PKG, with a key file you supply.
- Movies: DVD-Video to MKV, MP4 or ISO, CSS decrypted on the fly. Blu-ray to ISO, decrypted when its key is in your KEYDB.cfg.
- Music: CDs to FLAC, MP3, AAC, ALAC, Opus or WAV, tagged from MusicBrainz with the cover.
- Sega, NEC, SNK, 3DO, CD-i, Dreamcast, Xbox, Xbox 360, GameCube, Wii and Wii U discs, on drives that can read them. Xbox to XISO, GameCube to CISO or RVZ, Wii to WBFS or RVZ.
- Send rips to the console, a USB disk, SMB, NFS, FTP or RomM.
- Drive test: put in your discs one by one, then attach the zip to the drive report form.
- RipperPS on the TV, and a web UI at `http://<ps5-ip>:8790`.

### Good to know

- The PS5's own drive can't read CDs. Music, PS1 and other CD games need a USB drive.
- Xbox, GameCube, Wii, Wii U and Dreamcast need custom drive firmware (or a drive that reads GD-ROMs), and I haven't been able to try any of them on a real disc yet. That's what the drive test is for.
- PS4 and PS5 discs have to be installed before they can be dumped. Languages your copy doesn't include are left out, and the rip tells you which.
- PS5 dumps carry drakmor's pgo_stub and ampr_emu, which some games need to run from a folder or an image.
- Blu-ray movies need their key in a KEYDB.cfg, or the ISO stays encrypted. 4K discs (AACS 2) and BD+ aren't handled, and I haven't tried this on a real disc yet.
- Fake PKGs pass every check on the PC, but I haven't installed one on a console yet.
- XISO, CISO, RVZ and WBFS match what the usual tools write, but haven't been tried on real hardware.
- The background service doesn't start by itself after a reboot yet: open RipperPS again.
