The first public build of RipperPS, and it's a pre-release on purpose. Everything I've been able to test on my own console works, but the drive you plug in decides what can be ripped, and I don't own every drive out there. So this one is as much about gathering data as it is about ripping. If you have a drive with Kreon, OmniDrive or LibreDrive firmware, I'd love to hear from you.

### New

- Rip from the PS5's own Blu-ray drive and USB CD, DVD and Blu-ray drives, more than one at a time.
- Games: PS1 and PS2 to BIN/CUE or CHD, PS2 DVDs to ISO or CHD, PS3 discs to ISO (decrypted with a key file), PS4 and PS5 game dumps from installed games.
- Movies: DVD-Video to MKV, MP4 or ISO, CSS decrypted on the fly. Blu-ray to ISO.
- Music: CDs to FLAC, MP3, AAC, ALAC, Opus or WAV, tagged from MusicBrainz with the cover.
- Sega, NEC, SNK, 3DO, CD-i, Dreamcast, Xbox, Xbox 360, GameCube, Wii and Wii U discs, on drives that can read them.
- Send rips to the console, a USB disk, SMB, NFS, FTP or RomM.
- Drive test: put in your discs one by one, then attach the zip to the drive report form.
- RipperPS on the TV, and a web UI at `http://<ps5-ip>:8790`.

### Good to know

- The PS5's own drive can't read CDs. Music, PS1 and other CD games need a USB drive.
- Xbox, GameCube, Wii, Wii U and Dreamcast need custom drive firmware (or a drive that reads GD-ROMs), and I haven't been able to try any of them on a real disc yet. That's what the drive test is for.
- PS4 and PS5 discs have to be installed before they can be dumped. Games with parts that never got installed (other languages) can't be dumped yet.
- Blu-ray movies stay encrypted in the ISO for now.
- The background service doesn't start by itself after a reboot yet: open RipperPS again.
